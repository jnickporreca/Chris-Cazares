DEFEND Civil Defense

Defending Our Community. Protecting What Matters.

A civil lawsuit can threaten more than financial security. It can threaten a family’s stability, a person’s reputation, or a small business owner’s livelihood.

At Hawaiʻi Advocate, we believe community members and small business owners deserve experienced, dedicated representation when they are accused of civil wrongdoing or forced to defend themselves in litigation. We stand beside people who have something meaningful at stake.

Who We Defend

Community Members

Individuals facing civil lawsuits involving their property, finances, reputation, or personal responsibilities.

Small Business Owners

Entrepreneurs, professionals, and locally owned businesses confronting claims that may threaten their operations, assets, or future.

Our Approach

Every person deserves a fair opportunity to be heard, regardless of the resources available to the opposing party. Chris brings experience from both sides of the courtroom to the defense of community members and small businesses. His approach emphasizes preparation, careful investigation, practical judgment, and a willingness to take a case to trial when necessary.

Civil defense matters are accepted by referral only.

PROTECT Catastrophic Injury

Protecting People. Pursuing Accountability. Restoring Lives.

A catastrophic injury can change a person’s life in an instant. The consequences often extend beyond physical pain. They can affect a person’s independence, career, relationships, financial security, and ability to enjoy everyday life.

At Hawaiʻi Advocate, we represent individuals and families whose lives have been changed by serious injuries caused by negligence or wrongful conduct.

Our Approach

We believe an injury case should tell the complete story of what happened and how a person’s life has changed. That requires more than reviewing medical bills. It requires understanding the individual, the family, and the long-term consequences of the injury.

Chris brings extensive jury trial experience and a commitment to thorough case preparation, persuasive presentation, and meaningful accountability. Hawaiʻi Advocate welcomes referrals and co-counsel opportunities involving serious injury and wrongful death litigation.

All cases are considered by referral only.

ADVOCATE Co-Counsel & Attorney Training

Advocating Together. Strengthening Trial Lawyers.

The most significant cases often benefit from collaboration between experienced attorneys.

At Hawaiʻi Advocate, we work alongside other lawyers as co-counsel, trial counsel, and strategic partners in complex civil litigation. We also believe that trial advocacy is a craft that should be studied, practiced, and shared.

Co-Counsel and Trial Partnerships

Chris brings more than two decades of experience in criminal and civil litigation, including more than 100 jury trials. Hawaiʻi Advocate welcomes opportunities to collaborate with attorneys seeking additional courtroom experience, trial preparation assistance, or strategic support.

  • Co-counsel representation
  • Trial preparation and strategy
  • Case theory development
  • Jury selection and voir dire
  • Opening statements
  • Direct examination and cross-examination
  • Closing arguments
  • Demonstrative evidence and courtroom presentation
  • Complex liability and damages litigation

Our co-counsel relationships are grounded in mutual respect, clearly defined responsibilities, and a shared commitment to the client.

Attorney Training Program

Experience is most valuable when it is shared. Chris served as an instructor at the United States Air Force Judge Advocate General’s School, helping train fellow attorneys in trial advocacy and litigation skills. Through Hawaiʻi Advocate’s attorney training program, Chris continues that commitment by providing practical instruction designed to help attorneys improve their courtroom skills.

  • Developing persuasive case theories
  • Jury selection and connecting with jurors
  • Opening statements and storytelling
  • Direct examination
  • Cross-examination
  • Closing arguments
  • Effective courtroom communication
  • Trial preparation and presentation
  • Authenticity and confidence in the courtroom

Our goal is not to make every lawyer argue or present cases the same way. It is to help attorneys develop the skills, judgment, and confidence to become more effective advocates while remaining authentic to their individual styles.

About the Attorney

Trial Attorney. Advocate. Instructor.

Chris has dedicated his legal career to the practice of trial advocacy, serving as a prosecutor, criminal defense attorney, civil trial lawyer, and instructor to fellow attorneys.

A graduate of the University of Southern California Gould School of Law, Chris began his legal career in the United States Air Force Judge Advocate General’s Corps.

  1. Military Legal Career

    Chris served as a prosecutor at F.E. Warren Air Force Base in Cheyenne, Wyoming, before becoming an Area Defense Counsel, representing military service members facing criminal accusations and adverse proceedings. He subsequently served on the defense team in the military commission proceedings involving Abd al-Rahim al-Nashiri, accused in connection with the USS Cole bombing. Chris later served as a Senior Trial Counsel in the United States Air Force, prosecuting serious criminal cases and developing substantial courtroom experience.

  2. Teaching Trial Advocacy

    Chris also served as an instructor at the United States Air Force Judge Advocate General’s School. His instructional experience reflects a commitment to developing the courtroom skills of other attorneys. Through Hawaiʻi Advocate, Chris continues that work by collaborating with fellow lawyers and providing practical trial advocacy training.

  3. Private Practice and Civil Litigation

    Following his military legal assignments, Chris established a private criminal defense practice, representing individuals facing serious criminal allegations. His career subsequently expanded into plaintiff-side personal injury litigation, representing individuals harmed by negligence and wrongful conduct. Having worked as both a prosecutor and defense attorney, and later in civil litigation, Chris brings a broad perspective to evaluating evidence, developing trial strategy, and presenting cases to juries.

Philosophy of Advocacy

Chris believes that the role of a trial lawyer is fundamentally about standing beside people when the stakes are highest. Sometimes that means defending a community member or small business owner against a civil lawsuit. Sometimes it means pursuing accountability for a person whose life has been changed by catastrophic injury. And sometimes it means working alongside another attorney to prepare a case for trial or helping develop the skills of the next generation of advocates.

Whatever the circumstances, the commitment remains the same: preparation, integrity, independent judgment, and a willingness to stand up for the people who place their trust in him.
